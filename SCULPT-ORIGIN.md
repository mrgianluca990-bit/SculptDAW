# Sculpt DAW — origin and attribution

Sculpt DAW is an independent downstream adaptation of **SoundCraft**
(https://github.com/storytold/soundcraft), by the ArtCraft Team and SoundCraft contributors.
SoundCraft source files remain under their original **MIT OR Apache-2.0** license.
Original LICENSE-MIT, LICENSE-APACHE and NOTICE are preserved unchanged.
ATTRIBUTION.md is retained and extended with entries for the new Sculpt artwork.

Sculpt DAW adaptations and new artwork are separately authored for this project.
Do not reuse ArtCraft trademarks, wordmark or logos in Sculpt branding.

Import compatibility scripts in `sculpt/` are separate new modules. Their features
are experimental; no AAX host and no PTX session reader are supplied in this patch.

The native file format remains `.scraft` for backwards compatibility.
